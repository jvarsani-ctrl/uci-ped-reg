1Scope

UCI-Ped-Reg is a hospital-based paediatric cancer registry built on REDCap at the Uganda Cancer Institute. ICCC-3 coding is implemented with REDCap's native branching logic and @CALCTEXT calculated fields only.

This page reproduces, verbatim, the field labels, choices and expressions that assign the ICCC-3 extended classification and subgroup for Group X. In this group the result depends on two entered values: the ICD-O-3.2 morphology and the topography. The expressions run in your browser. Nothing you select is stored or transmitted.

Sections 5 and 6 show two further calculated fields from the same dictionary: age at the first UCI visit, and the location and level of the referring facility. Their values are shown for one illustrative entry each.

      5Age at the first UCI visit
      
  
      Age is not entered. Three calculated fields derive it from the date of birth on the Demographics instrument and the date of the first visit to UCI on the Referral characteristics instrument. age_days counts the days between the two dates; age_months and age_year divide that count by one twelfth of the Julian year (30.4375 days) and by the Julian year (365.25 days) and round down, so both express completed months and years.
      
  
      
    
      VariableValueSource or expression
      
    
      date_birth14-03-2019Entered (Demographics)
      visit_date02-06-2025Entered (Referral characteristics): first visit to UCI
      age_days2272datediff([date_birth], [visit_date], "d", "dmy", true)
      age_months74rounddown([age_days]/30.4375, 0)
      age_year6rounddown([age_days]/365.25, 0)
      
  
      
  
      Dates are illustrative and do not belong to a registered patient. Because age is anchored to the first UCI visit, it does not increase with time; it changes only if either date is corrected.
      

      


      
  
      6Location and level of the referring facility
      
  
      When a child is referred from a facility in Uganda, the abstractor selects it from ref_facility, a list of 3,346 facilities. The stored value is the facility's position in that list. Two calculated fields read that number:
      
  
        
    
      • facility_location returns one of 146 locations (136 districts and 10 cities). The expression is a binary search: 538 nested comparisons of the form [ref_facility]< n, of which any one facility passes through at most 10.
        • 
    
      • facility_level returns one of 6 levels of care from contiguous ranges of positions.
        •

      If a new choice were inserted at position 3 and the list renumbered, every record holding the stored value 3 would display the new choice, and iv_code would return its code. No record would have been edited. In a REDCap project in development status, such a change takes effect immediately and without a warning. The registry therefore applies three rules to positional lists:

      8Method
      
  
      Labels, choices and expressions were exported from the UCI-Ped-Reg REDCap data dictionary on 1 October 2026 through the REDCap API (metadata only; no records). The Group X expression x_extended is shown in its corrected form of 13 October 2026, in which the site conditions and the Xa5 morphology list follow the ICCC-3 (2017) definitions. The page evaluates them with a parser that follows REDCap's comparison rules: equality is numeric when both operands are numbers and textual otherwise, and an ordering comparison with a blank operand is false.
